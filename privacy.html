Who we are

This website belongs to Sina Tattooer, a private tattoo studio at Mr Snips Barbers, 23 Yarburgh Way, York, YO10 5HD. If you have any question about your information, email contact@sinatattooer.co.uk.

Cookies

Analytics cookies are only set if you choose Accept on the cookie banner. Nothing is used for advertising.

If you reject, Google Analytics may still receive a basic, cookieless signal that a page was viewed. Nothing is stored on your device and it can't be used to recognise you.

The site also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.

Some pages show content from other services: a Google Map on the home page, and an Instagram feed and Google reviews shown through Behold and EmbedSocial. These services may set their own cookies when they load. Their privacy policies explain how.

Enquiries and bookings

When you send an enquiry (through the online form, WhatsApp, Instagram, phone or email), I use your details and any images you send only to reply, give you a quote and arrange your appointment. The online form is run by Tally (tally.so), which passes your answers to me by email.

Only share health details, such as skin conditions or past reactions to ink, if you're happy for me to use them to plan your tattoo safely. You can always leave them for the consultation.

Enquiries that don't lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months. Your details are never sold or used for marketing.

Other services this site uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and protects it from attacks. It processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. No cookies are set.
  • Google Workspace handles the studio's email.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time by emailing contact@sinatattooer.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
