Blackwork is the studio's most-requested style — and one of the most rewarding. Solid black ink, confident linework and high-contrast composition give blackwork its impact, and because the design relies on depth of black rather than colour, it ages better than almost any other approach. Why blackwork ages well

Built to last

Solid black ink holds shape and contrast longer than any other pigment. A blackwork tattoo done well can look almost unchanged a decade in.

Cover-up friendly

Dense black is the most predictable way to hide an existing tattoo. If you're considering a cover-up, blackwork is usually the shortest path to a clean result.

Style range

From illustrative and geometric to ornamental and ignorant-style, blackwork is broad enough to build a full custom design around any concept.

Common questions about blackwork

  • Pain is mostly down to placement, not style. Solid black fills can feel slightly more intense than line-only work because the area is gone over more, but Sina paces sessions and uses numbing where appropriate. Most clients describe blackwork as comparable to other styles in the same area.

  • Surface healing takes around 2–3 weeks, and the deeper layers settle over 2–3 months. Solid black areas may peel more visibly during the first fortnight, which is normal. Follow the aftercare guide and the design ages exceptionally well.

  • Yes — blackwork is one of the most reliable cover-up approaches. Solid black or dense illustrative work hides previous tattoos predictably. Send a clear photo via the booking form and Sina will tell you what is realistic. See the cover-up gallery for examples.
