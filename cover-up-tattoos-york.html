Cover-ups are some of the most rewarding work in the studio. Whether it's an old name, a holiday tattoo that didn't go to plan, or a piece that just doesn't reflect who you are anymore — almost every existing tattoo can be reimagined into a custom design you'll actually want to wear. Sina specialises in cover-ups using blackwork, dense illustrative work and bold floral designs, all of which give the most predictable results.

Every cover-up starts with a confidential consultation. Send a clear, well-lit photo of the existing tattoo via the booking form and Sina will give you honest feedback on what's realistic — including whether a couple of laser sessions beforehand would give your final design more freedom. There's no judgement and no pressure: the goal is a result you're genuinely happy with, not a sale.

How a cover-up actually works

Honest assessment first

Before any design work, Sina reviews the existing tattoo and tells you what's realistic. If laser would help, you'll hear that upfront — not after a deposit.

Custom-built around what's there

Cover-up designs aren't picked from flash. Each one is custom-drawn so the new shapes and dark tones do the work of hiding the old piece naturally.

Full discretion

Every consultation is private. Photos and references stay confidential. The goal is a result you're proud of — Sina has covered everything from regretted names to faded holiday souvenirs.
