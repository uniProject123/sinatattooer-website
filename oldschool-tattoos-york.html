Old School & Traditional Tattoos in York

Bold outlines, classic imagery and timeless technique — American traditional tattooing by Sina in a private York studio.

Old school tattooing — or American traditional — is the foundation the whole craft was built on. Bold black outlines, a classic restricted palette, and recognisable iconography: roses, swallows, anchors, daggers, panthers, eagles, hearts. The style is defined by simplicity and legibility. Every element is intentional, every shape is clean, and the result holds on skin better than almost anything else you can get tattooed.

Sina works within the traditional aesthetic but all pieces are custom-designed. If you have a subject or theme in mind — even something that wouldn't typically be associated with old school tattooing — it can be interpreted in the style. Send your references and ideas through the booking form and Sina will sketch a custom design suited to your placement and size.

Why old school tattoos stand the test of time

Exceptional longevity

Bold outlines and solid colour blocks hold their shape and saturation longer than any other style. A well-executed traditional piece looks sharp decades later.

Instant readability

Old school designs are built to be understood at a glance. The simplified shapes and strong contrast make them legible on any skin tone at any scale.

Custom within tradition

The traditional aesthetic is a framework, not a formula. Any subject — personal, symbolic or purely aesthetic — can be interpreted in the style. No two pieces need to be the same.
